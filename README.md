# Muhammed Najeem & Fathima Mirsha — Wedding Invitation

A luxury interactive wedding invitation web application featuring the opening envelope animation, wax seal, falling & interactive rose petals, background music with volume fade-in, scratch-to-reveal wedding date card, live countdown timer, interactive guestbook wishes, Quranic verses, family details, and venue directions with Google Maps.

## ✨ Features & Animations

- **3D Envelope Opening Animation**: 
  - Envelope flap folds open in 3D (`rotateX(180deg)`).
  - Royal wax seal with "N & M" monogram stamp.
  - Smooth scale transitions with gold borders and vintage corner ornaments.
- **Audio Experience**: 
  - Automatic gentle volume fade-in when the envelope opens.
  - Floating gold-pulsing music toggle button (Play/Pause).
  - Authentic wedding invitation background audio.
- **Rose Petal Effects**:
  - Background drifting rose petals floating gently.
  - Interactive petal shower burst anywhere you tap or click on the screen.
- **Scratch-to-Reveal Date Card**:
  - Metallic gold scratch-off canvas (`✦ SCRATCH TO REVEAL DATE ✦`).
  - Scratching with mouse/finger reveals the date, month, year, and time with smooth auto-completion at 45% scratched.
- **Live Countdown Timer**:
  - Real-time countdown to Sunday, November 8, 2026 at 11:00 AM (Days, Hours, Minutes, Seconds).
- **Celebration & Venue Details**:
  - Sagar Auditorium, Kavumpuram, Valanchery, Kerala.
  - Direct "VENUE LOCATION" Google Maps integration button.
- **Family Blessings & Sacred Covenant**:
  - Groom: Muhammed Najeem (S/o Hamza & Ayisha, Chenakkattil (H), Vengad).
  - Bride: Fathima Mirsha (D/o Abdul Vahab & Sameera, Pullat (H), Vaniyanoor).
  - Holy Quran Surah Ar-Rum (30:21) & Prophetic Du'a in elegant Arabic calligraphy and English translation.
- **Guestbook Wishes & Blessings**:
  - Interactive form for guests to leave prayers and wishes.
  - Saved to local storage with edit and delete capabilities.
